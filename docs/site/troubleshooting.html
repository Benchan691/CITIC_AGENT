Quick triage

Most failures are one of six things: a missing environment variable, an unreachable external service, a stale build (tracked lib/), a stale/rejected session, a policy denial working as intended, or a genuinely denied operation. Start with ./setup.sh --check, then the area below.

Escalation evidence to collect

git rev-parse HEAD + branch; the failing command's full output; relevant log lines including soc_correlation_id; for policy surprises, the exact tool name and the admin Access & approvals state. Redact credentials and customer data before sharing.
